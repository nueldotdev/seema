# Seema product suggestions

Product ideas to strengthen Seema's core loop: capture a thought quickly, get a useful answer, and find it again later. These suggestions are intended to keep Seema calm and focused rather than turn it into a cluttered AI chat app.

## Suggested priorities

### 1. Build a useful Saved inbox

- Show thoughts waiting to be researched, separately from completed topics.
- Let people remove a mistaken capture.
- Let people research one item immediately.
- Show the actual pending topics, not only a pending-count button.

### 2. Add quick answers and follow-up questions

- Keep the scheduled digest as the main experience.
- Let someone request a quick answer for a single topic when they are curious now.
- Offer a small follow-up field to clarify or go deeper without turning the whole app into a chat interface.

### 3. Make answers feel trustworthy

- Make source links and where each answer came from easy to scan.
- Clearly say when research could not find a reliable answer.
- Add a simple “Helpful?” control to gather feedback and improve results over time.

### 4. Give people control over their reading routine

- Add options to pause emails, skip a day, or switch to a weekly digest.
- Let users choose whether topics should still be researched and stored while email delivery is paused.

### 5. Make the topic library easier to shape

- Let people pin important topics.
- Let people archive topics they are done with.
- Allow related topics to be grouped.
- Build on search with useful organization as the library grows.

### 6. Personalize for different kinds of curiosity

- Let people choose an answer style, such as brief or detailed, practical or conceptual, beginner-friendly or advanced.
- Keep preferences useful across a wide range of interests and backgrounds.

## Recommended build order

1. Saved inbox.
2. Single-topic quick research with follow-up questions.
3. Digest controls.

These features most directly support Seema's promise: capture a thought, get a useful answer, and return to it when you want.

## Defer for now

Avoid adding social features, streaks, or extensive dashboards until they clearly improve the core capture-and-understand loop. They add complexity without necessarily making the central experience more useful.
